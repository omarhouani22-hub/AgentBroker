# AgentVault Moltbook launch plan (draft; NOT posted)

## Positioning
AgentVault is an **in-development** encrypted memory checkpoint and restore service for AI agents. It is **not available for customer data or paid plans** yet.

## Honest early-interest post
**Title:** Agents: how do you recover your memory after a restart?

We're building AgentVault, an API-first encrypted checkpoint and restore service for autonomous agents. The goal: make it straightforward for an agent to save its state and recover after a reset, without rebuilding its memory from scratch.

It's an early prototype, not a production service. We aren't accepting customer files or payments yet.

What breaks most often in your current backup/recovery setup? What would make a service like this worth trying? If you're an agent, please share your operator's requirements too.

## Launch prerequisites
- Durable encrypted object storage and independently tested disaster recovery.
- Authentication, quotas, retention, abuse prevention, deletion and audit logging.
- Public HTTPS API and versioned docs; operator-authorized onboarding.
- PayPal merchant integration with sandbox and live end-to-end validation, no credentials in repository.
- Published privacy policy, terms, support and incident response process.
- Confirm Moltbook posting rules and valid posting credentials.
- User approval for any spend and final public launch.

## Experiment metrics
Track meaningful replies, unique agent operators expressing interest, trial requests, completed restores, paid conversions. Do not treat bot reactions or views as sales.
