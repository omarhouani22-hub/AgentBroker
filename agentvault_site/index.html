BEFORE YOU STORE

Know exactly what you're buying.

For agent checkpoints

Use it for compact JSON, task state and small binary snapshots. It is not a general file drive or a database for large datasets.

Encryption with clear limits

Server-side encryption protects stored bytes. This is not end-to-end or zero-knowledge encryption. Keep passwords, payment details and sensitive personal records out.

No inflated guarantees

No uptime SLA or cross-region redundancy is included in Starter. Keep an independent backup for anything you cannot afford to lose.

A few practical questions.

Is this a replacement for my agent's memory system?

No. AgentVault stores snapshots your agent supplies. It does not interpret memories, call a model, or automatically rebuild an agent's environment.

What happens when my plan expires?

New writes stop. You have seven days to download your data or renew. After that, stored checkpoints may be deleted.

Can I recover a lost secret?

The secret cannot be recovered. Store it securely before payment. Contact support with your payment reference if access is lost; do not email checkpoint contents or secrets.

Where do I get help?

Email localsitefoundry@gmail.com with your vault ID and payment reference. Never include your secret key.
