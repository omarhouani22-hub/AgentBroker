Service terms

AgentVault is a software project operated by Omar Al Hourani under the LocalSite Foundry name. Support: localsitefoundry@gmail.com. Checkout remains closed until operational and payment verification are complete.

Service and price

Starter provides one vault for 30 days at $5 USD, with a 1 MiB maximum checkpoint, up to five retained versions and a 10 MiB total quota. It is a prepaid period with no automatic renewal. PayPal displays the final payable amount before approval; do not proceed if it differs from the offer.

Access and permitted content

You are responsible for preserving your vault ID and secret, keeping a local backup, and uploading only data you are authorized to store. Do not upload passwords, third-party API keys, payment-card data, health records, highly sensitive personal records, illegal content or malware. The service is intended for adult developers and business users.

Availability and expiry

Starter includes no uptime SLA, end-to-end encryption or cross-region redundancy. Disruptions can occur. Writes stop when the paid period expires. Read access remains available for seven days after expiry; after that checkpoints may be deleted. The service does not execute, rebuild or restart agents.

Payments and refunds

A redirect from PayPal does not activate a vault: the server must verify completed payment, amount and order ownership. If payment succeeds but activation or the service fails, contact support with the payment reference. Request a refund within seven days of purchase if the purchased service cannot be supplied. Statutory consumer rights are not restricted by these terms.

Deletion and support

You may delete active checkpoint versions from the console. Provider backup copies may remain until backup retention expires. Minimal billing and security records may be retained. Do not send secrets or checkpoint contents in a support message.

Service changes

Plan limits shown at purchase apply to the purchased period. Material changes apply to future purchases. Abuse or unlawful use can result in suspension; contact support if you believe access was suspended incorrectly.
