Privacy notice

AgentVault is operated by Omar Al Hourani under the LocalSite Foundry project. For privacy questions or data requests, contact localsitefoundry@gmail.com. Do not include secrets or checkpoint contents.

Data the service processes

The application stores a vault identifier, a hash of your access secret, encrypted checkpoint bytes, checkpoint sizes, versions, timestamps, integrity digests, accepted terms version, plan expiry and payment order references. It stores minimal security events. It does not need your model prompts or third-party API keys.

Payments

PayPal processes payment information under its own privacy terms. AgentVault verifies the order server-to-server and stores order identifiers, amount and payment state. Payment responses may contain payer information transiently; the application does not persist full responses or card details.

Infrastructure and encryption

Hosting and database providers process the data required to run the service, which may involve international transfers. Production provider names, locations and backup retention must be disclosed before sales are enabled. Stored checkpoint content is encrypted server-side; the service holds the decryption key. Encryption does not make this a zero-knowledge service.

Your browser

The console uses session storage to retain the vault ID, secret and pending order reference within the browser tab. Disconnect clears these entries. No advertising or third-party analytics scripts are included in the AgentVault frontend. Hosting providers can retain operational logs such as IP addresses and request timestamps.

Retention and deletion

Only the latest five checkpoint versions are kept in active storage. After the paid period expires, a seven-day export window applies and checkpoints may then be deleted. Console deletion removes active versions; provider backup copies expire under provider retention rules. Billing references may be retained for accounting, disputes and fraud prevention.

Your choices

You can download checkpoints, delete active checkpoint versions, disconnect the browser and contact support to request deletion of remaining account metadata, subject to legally required record retention. Do not upload sensitive personal records.
